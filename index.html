What you get

  • A chat button on your website that answers your most common questions (timings, prices, address, services, your own FAQs).
  • Understands English and Roman Urdu, and forgives small spelling mistakes.
  • A short enquiry form inside the chat (name, preferred time, what they need) that opens WhatsApp with the message already written. The customer presses send.
  • Your brand colour, your business name, works on phones.
  • Nothing to maintain on a server. It is plain files, so it can be hosted free (for example on GitHub Pages). There is no monthly server bill.
  • Changes to prices or hours are a small edit to one text file.

How it works

  1. You send me your details.Timings, address, services with prices, and the questions customers ask most.
  2. I set up your chat.I write your questions and answers, set your colours and WhatsApp number, and test it.
  3. One line goes on your site.A single script tag is added to your website. Enquiries then arrive on your WhatsApp.

What it is not

It is not a general AI. It answers the questions it has been set up for and is good at those. When it does not know, it says so and offers to pass the message to you on WhatsApp. It does not send anything by itself: the customer always presses send in WhatsApp.
